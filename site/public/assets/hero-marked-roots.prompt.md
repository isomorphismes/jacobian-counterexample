> infinity, elegant and intellectually serious, not sci-fi spectacle. Palette:
> midnight navy and ink blue background, luminous teal/cyan strands,
> restrained coral and warm gold accents, soft ivory highlights. Fine grain,
> precise vector-like curves with painterly atmospheric depth. Reserve
> generous clean dark negative space on the left third for overlaid headline
> copy; visual focus on center-right. No text, no equations, no letters, no
> numbers, no logos, no watermark, no people, no literal paper or blackboard.
> High-end scientific magazine cover aesthetic, accessible contrast, cohesive
> with a modern academic website.

The artwork is conceptual. It is not a mathematical plot of the map and should
not be used as evidence for any claim. Exact real-slice plots are generated
separately from the displayed polynomial formula.
